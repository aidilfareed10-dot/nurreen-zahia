The Firm

Built on trust.
Driven by tenacity.

Est.2016

Chambers at Seksyen 9, Shah Alam — moments from the Shah Alam Court Complex.

Nurreen Zahia & Associates was founded on a simple conviction: every client — from established corporations to individuals and families — deserves legal counsel of the highest calibre.

With over ten years of experience, our practice is grounded in professionalism, practical advice and a commitment to meaningful outcomes. From our chambers in Shah Alam we serve clients throughout Malaysia, and act for international clients and businesses who need a trusted partner on the ground in Malaysia.

Our advocates are enrolled practitioners of the High Court of Malaya with rights of audience before the Malaysian courts and tribunals. We aim to understand each client's objectives and deliver clear, effective, results-driven solutions.
